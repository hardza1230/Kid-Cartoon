# Imagegen prompts and invariants

Built-in imagegen was used for these three project assets. These are the production specifications used to generate them, recorded for future matching episodes.

## Kitten atlas

Illustration-story: square sprite sheet, exactly 4 columns × 4 rows, 16 equal cells, same adorable orange tabby kitten in every cell. Full body sitting facing camera, thick warm dark brown outlines, flat pastel cel shading, cream muzzle and tummy, pink inner ears, big friendly eyes. Consistent scale and baseline, transparent margins, no borders, labels or text. Row 1 neutral / half blink / closed blink / open eyes; row 2 head tilts; row 3 raises paw and waves; row 4 happy open mouth / smaller mouth / closed smile / neutral. Genuine transparent background. Requested 2048×2048; use the actual generated dimensions when dividing the atlas.

## Background

Illustration-story: portrait 9:16 preschool vocabulary playroom, mint wall, cream rounded window and blue sky, peach floor, small pastel plant at bottom left, low toy shelf at bottom right. Large empty center for the kitten and empty upper center for title. Flat clean 2D cartoon, warm brown soft outlines, coral/mint/butter-yellow palette. No characters, words or logos.

## Decoration atlas

Illustration-story: 2×2 equal cells on a transparent square canvas. Golden sparkle star, coral/mint confetti, empty mint speech bubble, peach heart. Isolated decorations, warm brown outlines, centered in each cell with generous padding. No text, grid lines or characters.
